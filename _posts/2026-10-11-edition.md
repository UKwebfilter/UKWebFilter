## 1. Man killed by a tiger after climbing into its enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park

A man has died after entering the tiger enclosure at Yorkshire Wildlife Park near Doncaster on Saturday afternoon. South Yorkshire Police said they were called at 1.35pm to reports of a man entering the enclosure, and that despite the efforts of medical staff he was pronounced dead at the scene. The force said the tiger was uninjured, stayed inside the enclosure and that there was no wider risk to the public, and it urged people not to share "distressing" footage circulating online. The park was evacuated and will stay closed on Sunday.

The man has not been named. The Mail on Sunday, citing witnesses, says he was thought to be in his 20s and that keepers fired acoustic guns to drive off the tiger, which it names as Altai; the New York Times describes it as an Amur tiger. According to the BBC's review of the papers, the attack leads the Sunday Mirror, the Sunday People and the Daily Star Sunday.

## 3. Burnham pressed to distance Labour from Manchester City after MPs' freebies are revealed

The Mail on Sunday, drawing on the MPs' Register of Interests and a Sun analysis, says Labour MPs received almost £290,000 in gifts over a decade, including £14,000 of City tickets and hospitality and £275,000 in flights and trips to the UAE taken by 25 Labour MPs. It reports that Tory MPs accepted nearly £175,000 of UAE trips, and that the SNP, Lib Dems and Reform accounted for £18,500.

The Sun says Labour's deputy leader Lucy Powell, whose constituency includes the Etihad Stadium, accepted tickets worth £2,360 to five matches, and that the Conservatives have urged Burnham to publish his correspondence and financial links with the club. ## 4. Camden council was warned about a paedophile nursery worker years before his arrest

The Guardian reports that Vincent Chan, a nursery worker jailed in February after pleading guilty to 56 offences including assault by penetration on three-year-old girls at a Bright Horizons nursery on Finchley Road in north-west London, had been the subject of an earlier safeguarding investigation. It says "significant concerns" were raised with the nursery in 2021 by the parent of a young girl. Bright Horizons referred the matter to Camden council, which advised the nursery to investigate; Chan was put on duties with no contact with children and then cleared to return to his role.

A year later, in 2022, Chan filmed himself abusing three-year-old girls at the nursery, according to the Guardian. He was first arrested in June 2024 on suspicion of child cruelty after staff found videos of distressed children, and police then found tens of thousands of child abuse images on his devices. Prosecutors described him as a "prolific sexual predator".

The Northern Lights have been seen across the UK on Friday and Saturday evenings, with sightings as far south as Devon, Hampshire, Kent and Lyme Regis. ## 6. Houthi attack on Riyadh airport kills 12 and injures more than 300

At least 12 people were killed and 309 injured in an attack on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Saturday, the Saudi civil aviation authority said. The dead included four Saudis, two Bangladeshis and one person each from the US, Jordan, Palestine, Syria, Sudan and Egypt. A missile struck the complex housing domestic terminals 3 and 4, a regional official told the Associated Press, according to the Guardian.
