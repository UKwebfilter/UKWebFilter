## 1. Harry Kane ends England's 25-year wait for a men's Ballon d'Or winner after a record-breaking season

Harry Kane has become the first English player to win the Ballon d'Or since Michael Owen in 2001, after being crowned the world's best player at a ceremony at the Palladium in London on Monday night, the BBC reported. The 33-year-old Bayern Munich striker is the fifth Englishman to win the award, after Stanley Matthews, Bobby Charlton and Kevin Keegan. Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappe finished second and Barcelona's Lamine Yamal third.

Kane scored 61 goals in 51 club matches last season, the most a Bayern player has struck in one campaign, and 73 goals in 65 matches including England games. He won the award despite losing in both the World Cup and Champions League semi-finals, and is the first Bayern player to win since Karl-Heinz Rummenigge in 1981. Kane said he "was never the person that people thought was going to be the superstar". England manager Thomas Tuchel said he was "delighted", and Prince William called Kane a "brilliant captain". The Wembley arches were lit in gold. Spain's Alexia Putellas won the women's award for a record-equalling third time, France 24 and the Guardian reported. The Daily Express said fans tried to break into the ceremony, adding it was unclear whether any arrests were made.

## 2. Healey promises tariff cuts on more than 100 staple goods and an end to the carer's allowance cliff edge ahead of Wednesday's Budget

The government said the changes could save households up to £21 a year when combined with existing tariff suspensions. The measures include ending the so-called cliff edge for people receiving carer's allowance, replacing the earnings limit with a taper, which the Guardian said followed its investigation into penalties imposed on unpaid carers.

Other measures include making a 55p per mile tax-free mileage rate permanent and two weeks of paternity pay for self-employed fathers.
