## 1. Russell Brand labelled a ruthless sexual predator as his rape trial opens

Russell Brand was described by prosecutors as a "ruthless", "calculated" and "manipulative" sexual predator as his trial for rape and sexual assault opened at Southwark Crown Court. The 51-year-old actor and comedian faces three counts of rape, three of sexual assault and one of indecent assault, relating to six women and spanning 1999 to 2009. He denies all the charges.

Riel Karmy-Jones KC told jurors the women did not know each other and came forward independently, and that Brand's desire for sexual contact was "indiscriminate and voracious". The jury heard allegations including rape after a 1999 Labour conference fringe event, assaults at MTV and Capital Radio, an oral rape in a Soho bar toilet, and an anal rape at his home after a Hammersmith Apollo show. Brand's former flatmate Mark Pinheiro said Brand told him he may have gone "a bit too far" with a woman in Bournemouth. Brand told police he had never engaged in non-consensual activity. Thursday's papers led with the prosecution's "predator" language: the Mail called him the "predator in sunglasses", the Sun and Mirror used "predator", and the i Paper put the opening day on its front.

## 2. Zelensky says he feels betrayed by Trump as Starlink limits hit Ukraine's drones

Volodymyr Zelensky, on a visit to London, said he felt betrayed by Donald Trump after Ukraine's military reported sudden speed limits on SpaceX's Starlink network that disrupted drone and air-defence missions. Overnight, terminals travelling faster than about 100mph lost connectivity on the military-linked Starshield system, which Elon Musk said is owned and controlled by the US government, with any limits decided by the US military and ultimately the president. Zelensky told Piers Morgan the decision would bring "a lot of human losses" and that "people will die".

The outage came as Russia launched a major strike with ballistic missiles and drones, leaving about 600,000 people without power, and as allies again gave no advance warning of incoming missiles.
